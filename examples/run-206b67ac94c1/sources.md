### s_urban_personal — Manufactured Housing Personal Property Loans {#s-urban-personal}

### s_urban_role — The Role of Manufactured Housing in Increasing the Supply of Affordable Housing {#s-urban-role}

- **Tier:** 2 (think_tank)
- **Publisher:** Urban Institute
- **Publication date:** 2022-07-01
- **Access date:** 2026-08-19
- **Jurisdiction:** United States
- **Synthetic fixture:** no
- **URL:** https://www.urban.org/sites/default/files/2022-07/The%20Role%20of%20Manufactured%20Housing%20in%20Increasing%20the%20Supply%20of%20Affordable%20Housing.pdf
- **Safety flags:** Issuer-provided transaction-performance data cited in the report should not be generalized without independent validation.

> Using 2018–21 HMDA, the report shows 83.4–87.3 percent of chattel purchase lending was portfolio-held and only 0.2–0.4 percent was GSE-purchased. It argues GSE entry could improve availability and affordability but states chattel is riskier and requires scalable standardization, credit and servicing policies, monitoring, and potentially private risk transfer. It also reports limited private-label securitization and issuer-provided transaction performance.
